E-commerce Sales Analysis 📊

Project Overview
This project analyzes e-commerce sales data using Python and Power BI to understand sales performance, profit distribution, and business trends. It helps identify which product categories perform best, how sales change over time, and how different regions contribute to profit.

Tools Used
  Python
  Pandas
  Seaborn
  Power BI

Analysis Performed
  Sales by Category: Compared sales across Furniture, Office Supplies, and Technology.
  Profit by Region: Analyzed the percentage of profit contributed by the Central, East, South, and West regions.
  Sales by Month: Compared sales across the 12 months to identify higher and lower sales periods.
  Sales by Year: Analyzed yearly sales trends from 2015 to 2018.
  Regional Sales Comparison: Compared sales performance across different regions.

Key Findings
  Technology generated the highest sales among the three product categories.
  The South region contributed the largest share of profit, at approximately 26.3%.
  November recorded the highest monthly sales, while February recorded the lowest in the analysis shown.
  Yearly sales increased significantly from 2016 to 2018, reaching their highest level in 2018.
  Sales performance varied across regions, with the South and East showing relatively high average sales in the regional comparison.

Project Outcome
This project helped me develop practical skills in data cleaning, data analysis, data visualization, and dashboard creation. The analysis presents business data in a simple visual format, making it easier to understand sales performance and identify useful business insights.
